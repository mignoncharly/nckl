## Customer Workflow Proposal

Recommendation.

1. Customer chooses service: parcel shipping, shopping assistance, travel-agency pickup, or Europe shopping procurement.
2. Customer selects route direction: Germany/Europe -> Cameroon, Cameroon -> Germany/Europe, or local Cameroon shopping assistance.
3. Customer enters origin/drop-off location or pickup method.
4. Customer selects destination country/city or delivery area.
5. Customer selects accepted item categories and sees route-specific warnings.
6. If `phone` is selected, customer must confirm battery removed and route compatibility.
7. If small household equipment is selected, customer enters weight and sees the 31 kg maximum rule.
8. If shopping assistance from Europe is selected, customer submits product details/link, quantity, budget, delivery address in Cameroon, and WhatsApp contact.
9. Customer receives reference code and WhatsApp CTA.
10. Customer can track status and receive schedule/departure/arrival updates.

## Administrator Workflow Proposal

Recommendation.

1. Manage service catalogue and accepted item categories.
2. Manage route directions and route-specific conditions.
3. Manage drop-off locations, hours, contacts, and active/inactive state.
4. Manage shipment schedules with drop-off deadlines, departure dates, arrival dates, origin, destination, and notes.
5. Review customer requests, confirm service, route, item conditions, price, and payment status.
6. For shopping assistance, track purchase status, customer approval/payment, purchase completion, shipment, and doorstep delivery.
7. Send notifications for drop-off deadlines, departure, arrival, and status changes.
8. Export requests and schedules.
